SUSTAINABILITY + CULTURE + COMMUNITY + FUN!

About
Noraebang is the first Korean style singing booth (noraebang / private karaoke) in Finland, where you can sing alone or with a few other friends in a private space, without any pressure of performing for an audience or need to drink alcohol.

Its structure was created using a converted Finnish wooden sauna (birch wood) and soundproofed through bio-based materials like wood-fiber foam (created by Circular Foams) and mycelium.

Mycelium soundproofing panels were designed through participatory workshops using 3D-printed tiles inspired by 한글 / Hangul (Korean writing system) at BioMakers Studio. Over 50 people from Aalto community and beyond participated.

Little back story: Jin, Shawn, and Heya--art and design students at Aalto from Korea--started the project last year after being fascinated by Finland's love for karaoke and sauna while also feeling nostalgic for a cozy, Korean-style singing booth. We started to ponder how to recreate a Korean noraebang with a Finnish flavor and integrate the principle of sustainability. While doing so, some of our friends joined along the way and we are so proud of this project!
